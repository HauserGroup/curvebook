# History

Frozen records of finished CurveBook work. Nothing here describes current behaviour or open work. For that, read [`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) (claims and pending items) and [`STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md) (verified capabilities and known limits).

Files in this folder keep the counts, branch names, and wording they had when they were archived. Fix broken links in them, but do not update their numbers or rewrite their conclusions.

| Record | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) and [`roadmap/`](roadmap/) | Progress log and completed roadmap items, one file per milestone (`r0.md`–`r13.md`). Active items stay in [`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md). |
| [`legacy-ids-and-defects.md`](legacy-ids-and-defects.md) | Mapping of legacy `G1`–`G14`, `Slice 0`, `S1`, `S2`, and `M0`–`M8+` IDs onto roadmap items, plus the `#known-defects` table used to deduplicate audit findings. |

| [`audit/`](audit/) | Finished code-audit rounds: Round 1 progress and findings, the Round 2 delta audit, and the R0–R9 milestone review ledger. The reusable playbook stays in [`docs/audit/`](../audit/README.md).
