The original R10 phase plan records the intended execution order. An item listed both in an earlier phase and in Phase 7 lands in the earlier phase; Phase 7 enforces its gate. `R10.12` is split: its grid-side linked-region and exclusion styling belong to Phase 4, its `Import & Paste Special` dialog to Phase 5.
