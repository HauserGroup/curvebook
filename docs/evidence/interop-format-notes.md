# Interop Format Evidence Ledger (`.pzfx` and `.prism`)

This tracked ledger records every structural fact CurveBook relies on for reading and writing `.pzfx` and `.prism` files, together with its source, confidence level, and dependent subsystems. Detailed per-file hashed observations and dialect profiles live in the gitignored local ledger (`plan/format-observations/`, generated by `cargo xtask interop-corpus --observe`).

**Clean-room boundary:** This document records structural observations and public standards only. It contains zero corpus filenames, zero user titles or cell values, and zero vendor prose.
